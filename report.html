SUBJECT REFERENCE: █████████ / SCP-4373 (RETROSPECTIVE ASSOCIATION)

EVENT SUMMARY

No one remembers who ordered the execution.

By the time anyone tried to trace it back, the records were already inconsistent. Names changed between documents, dates refused to align, and the reason itself seemed to shift depending on who was looking at it. But one thing remained constant.

The outcome was supposed to be absolute.

The target was █████████. At the time, they were nothing special. No authority, no influence, no visible importance. Just another person in the wrong place, at the wrong time, caught in something far larger than themselves.
